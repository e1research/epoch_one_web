Student-Led AI Ecosystem

Advancing AI & Data Science Innovation at BRAC University

Epoch One is a collective committed to cultivating high-caliber Artificial Intelligence practitioners, conducting research, developing novel architectures, and delivering real-world community solutions.

The Gap We Address

While competitive programming and software development flourish across Bangladeshi universities, a noticeable void remains in structured Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. Epoch One was created to bridge this divide through mentorship, peer research groups, and rigorous technical culture.

Hands-on Execution

We aim to focus on developing proof-of-concept, standard AI/ML solutions. Our members engage through continuous learning, competitive internal assessments, curated dataset generation, and hybrid model exploration to solve real community problems.

Collaborative Vision

Collaboration is at the core of innovation. We try to unite students across disciplines who lack peers for hackathons, research publications, or startup ventures—building Bangladesh's most impactful undergraduate AI community.

News April 2026

Epoch One Members Publish IEEE Research on AI-Driven Earthquake Mainshock Classification

Following recent high-magnitude tremors across Bangladesh that triggered widespread public anxiety over potential after-effects and larger subsequent quakes, Epoch One members took action to address this crisis through data-driven research. Their resulting paper, now officially published in IEEE Xplorer, introduces a machine learning framework to rapidly distinguish whether a seismic event is a foreshock or a mainshock, a capability for minimizing public panic and activating timely disaster response. Trained on an 88-year USGS catalog with physics-informed features (including local seismicity rates and cumulative energy release), the team’s Bayesian-optimized CatBoost model achieved a 0.965 AUC and 0.97 recall for safety-critical detection, supported by SHAP interpretability to explain seismic stress predictors. This publication exemplifies Epoch One’s core purpose: empowering BRAC University students to conduct real-world AI research for pressing community challenges, turning local crises into peer-reviewed, globally recognized scientific contributions.
